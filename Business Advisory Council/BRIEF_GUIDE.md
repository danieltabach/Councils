# Brief Guide — Business Advisory Council

The **brief** is the single most important input besides the business materials themselves. It tells all 8 advisors what kind of feedback you need. Without it, they give a generic review. With it, every dollar spent produces targeted, actionable output.

## What the brief should contain

- **Review type**: What kind of review? (Full audit, positioning check, conversion review, brand review, market validation, financial reality check)
- **Stage**: Pre-launch, soft launch, early sales, growing, pivoting, scaling?
- **Goals**: What are you trying to figure out?
- **Concerns**: What specifically worries you? What needs extra attention?
- **Constraints**: Budget, team size (2 people — 1 PT, 1 FT), timeline, skill gaps
- **Context**: Background not in the materials (prior feedback, failed experiments, revenue numbers, etc.)

## Brief length guidelines

- 1-2 sentences: too short, gets generic output
- **1 structured paragraph (100-500 tokens): ideal** — costs < $0.01 extra
- Multiple pages: diminishing returns, dilutes focus

## Example briefs

### "Is our brand positioning clear?"
> REVIEW TYPE: Positioning and messaging check. STAGE: Pre-launch, finalizing brand assets. GOALS: Make sure a first-time visitor understands what we sell and why we're different in under 5 seconds. CONCERNS: We keep getting feedback that people "love the vibe" but can't articulate what we actually sell. CONSTRAINTS: 2-person team, launching in 6 weeks, can't do a full rebrand — need tactical fixes.

### "Why aren't we converting?"
> REVIEW TYPE: Conversion audit. STAGE: 3 months post-launch, ~200 visitors/day, <1% conversion rate. GOALS: Find the biggest leaks in our funnel and fix them this month. CONCERNS: Traffic is growing but sales are flat. Cart abandonment rate is 78%. CONSTRAINTS: Shopify store, limited dev skills, $500/month marketing budget. CONTEXT: We've tried discount popups and they didn't help.

### "Full business audit"
> REVIEW TYPE: Complete business evaluation. STAGE: 6 months in, $12K total revenue, not yet profitable. GOALS: Honest assessment — should we keep going, pivot, or shut down? CONCERNS: Unit economics might not work at current pricing. Getting traffic but repeat purchase rate is low. CONSTRAINTS: $8K remaining runway, both founders juggling other responsibilities. CONTEXT: Started as a passion project, trying to decide if it can become a real business.

### "Market positioning before launch"
> REVIEW TYPE: Market and competitive analysis. STAGE: Pre-launch, products designed but not manufactured. GOALS: Validate that there's a gap in the market for our positioning. CONCERNS: Three established brands seem to target the same customer. What's our edge? CONSTRAINTS: Can pivot product line before placing first manufacturing order.

### "Brand identity review"
> REVIEW TYPE: Visual and brand identity review. STAGE: Have logo, color palette, and 20 product photos. Building Shopify store. GOALS: Make sure our visual identity communicates premium quality without looking pretentious. CONCERNS: Not sure if our photography style matches our price point. Packaging design not started yet. CONSTRAINTS: Can't afford a professional photoshoot right now — using in-house photos.

## What NOT to put in the brief

- Full product catalogs or inventory lists (summarize the product line)
- Raw analytics exports (share key metrics: traffic, conversion rate, AOV, revenue)
- Entire email threads (pull out the key feedback points)
- Requests to "be harsh" or "be nice" (advisors have fixed mandates)
- Your full business plan (share the relevant sections in the materials file instead)
