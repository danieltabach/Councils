### 02 BRAND BACKGROUND

#### The Journey of Rosa Khaimova

**Origin Story**

Founded by Rosa Khaimova, Pressure Point Apparel embodies her journey from being overlooked, struggling with mental and physical health issues, to embracing pressure as a privilege, directly shaping the brand's identity and mission.

**Why Pressure Point Was Born**

This brand didn't come from a business plan, it came from lived experience. It's made for the grapplers who understand the value of pressure. The ones who aren't necessarily the highest level in their craft, but still show up, embrace the hard moments, and understand what the mats demand. Pressure Point is the apparel version of that mindset — quiet, intentional, identity-driven.

#### Snakes & Pressure — Symbolism in Combat Sports

**Snake Control:** A snake's strike symbolizes precision and patience, mirroring the grappling philosophy that emphasizes composure over chaos. Strategic control in high-pressure moments leads to impactful outcomes on the mat.

**Pressure Points:** Pressure points demonstrate that precise control can create outsized impact in grappling and life, emphasizing the importance of focused technique and strategy.

#### Positioning Statement

To grapplers in search of identity-driven training apparel, only Pressure Point gives you minimalist, practitioner-built gear that represents composure and perseverance, on and off the mats.

#### Brand Essence and Promise

**Brand Promise:** Pressure Point Apparel builds thoughtfully designed apparel (for inside and outside of the gym) that reflects control, perseverance, and the understanding of pressure.

**Brand Essence:** Our brand essence embodies Controlled Intensity, emphasizing calmness and focus amidst the pressures of combat sports training.

## Our Story (Website Copy)

**Where It Started**

Pressure Point Apparel was born on the mats.

Founded by Rosa Khaimova, a BJJ practitioner, this brand came from lived experience — from being overlooked, from struggling mentally and physically, and from learning that the hardest moments are the ones that build you. The mats taught Rosa how to persevere. She got smashed by people bigger and more experienced, and she came back every single time. That cycle of pressure and persistence became the foundation for everything this brand stands for.

Pressure Point exists to carry that lesson forward.

**The Problem**

Most combat sports brands shout. Loud graphics, aggressive imagery, and designs that feel like they were made for a cage walk-out, not a Tuesday night open mat. The industry decided that grappling culture had to look angry.

We disagreed.

Pressure Point is for the grapplers who show up, embrace the grind, and don't need to announce it. The ones who understand that composure wins more than chaos ever will.

**The Snake**

Our symbol is a coiled snake — not because it's aggressive, but because it's patient. A snake doesn't waste energy. It waits, it reads the situation, and it strikes with precision when the moment is right. That's the same discipline that separates a composed grappler from a reactive one.

It represents control over chaos.

**What We Make**

We design clean, intentional apparel for inside and outside the gym. Every piece is produced in small batches because we'd rather get it right than get it out fast. No gimmicks, no filler, no generic combat cliches.

Our design philosophy is minimalist on purpose. The gear speaks for itself because the people who wear it do the same.

**Who It's For**

Pressure Point is for the grapplers who persevere. The ones who got submitted, pinned, and beaten, and still came back the next day. You don't have to be a world champion or a ten-year veteran. You just have to keep showing up.

If you've ever dealt with imposter syndrome on the mats, felt like you didn't belong, or wondered if you were tough enough to stick with it — this brand was built for you. Not the loudest in the room, just the ones who never left.

**Beyond the Mats**

A portion of every sale goes toward mental health initiatives because the pressure off the mats is just as real as the pressure on them. The grappling community deserves a brand that acknowledges that.

It's a deeply personal commitment.

**The Future**

We're just getting started. Every drop, every collaboration, and every piece we put out will carry the same philosophy we started with: composure over chaos, perseverance over ego, and the belief that the people who embrace pressure are the ones who grow from it.

**Pressure isn't a problem to escape — it's a privilege to earn.**

3. Which product, vendor, quantity, and price scenario gives us the best risk-adjusted outcome?

### Key Dashboard Features

- CEO Overview with alerts and financial cards
- Drop Planning Workbench for modeling new product purchases
- Vendor Quote Comparison
- Monthly budget vs actual tracking
- Inventory and sell-through analysis
- Subscription tracking
- Charity donation planning (10% of gross profits)
- Profit simulator for break-even analysis

### Dashboard Decision Rules

- Do not approve a drop if break-even units exceed expected sell-through units
- Do not approve a drop if projected cash after commitments is deeply negative
- Be cautious if expected profit is positive only at 100% sell-through
- Raise price, lower quantity, or get better vendor quote if true margin is below target
- Avoid heavy discounts on products with already-thin gym margins
- Treat slow/overstocked inventory as cash already tied up
