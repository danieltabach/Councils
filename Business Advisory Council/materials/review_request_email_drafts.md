## Notes for Implementation

**Recommended sequence in Judge.me:**
1. First request ({{ n_days_ago }} = 7-10 days after delivery): Use Drafts 1-7 (pick whichever fits your tone preference)
2. Second request / reminder ({{ n_days_ago }} = 14-21 days): Use Draft 8

**Tips:**
- Disable email confirmation for reviewers so the process is truly one-tap
- Photo/video reviews are enabled on Judge.me free plan — the templates don't need to mention it, the review form handles it
- Draft 4 (mission-driven) is strong for customers who found you through the AFSP connection or the "Our Origin" page
- Draft 5 (ultra-minimal) matches the brand voice most tightly — test this against a longer version and see which gets more responses
- Draft 6 (founder voice) is best for early customers where the personal connection matters most

**Brand voice reminders:**
- Confident, bold, reassuring. Never cocky.
- Short, impactful sentences. No corporate jargon.
- Composure in communication. Speak with conviction.
- The customer is a practitioner, not a "valued customer." Talk to them like a training partner.
